What stays in this tab?

Public client IDs and a temporary Microsoft login verifier use tab session storage. Access tokens and receipts stay in memory. Other projects under this GitHub Pages origin share its browser security boundary. This is a demo for an account and registration you control.
BRING YOUR OWN RECEIPTS

Import from any inbox.

Choose one plain-text .eml receipt, or a JSON array of receipts with from, subject, date and body. Maximum 2 MB and 500 receipts. Import replaces the current results.

Multipart .eml files aren't supported yet. Receipt contents stay in this browser tab. No AI service receives them.

Demo and imported receipts are processed in this tab. When you connect your own app, the browser requests read-only access and talks directly to Google or Microsoft APIs. SubFinder does not run a mailbox backend, upload receipts to an AI service, or request client secrets.
Google’s sign-in library is loaded when you choose Gmail setup. Your provider handles sign-in and consent. Client IDs and a short-lived Microsoft PKCE verifier are held in tab session storage; access tokens and receipt data are held in memory. Disconnect clears SubFinder’s local connection and workspace. Reload discards the token and receipts. Remove access in your Google or Microsoft account settings to revoke the grant itself.
